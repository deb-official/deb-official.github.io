Characterize Then Distill

Mechanistic Reasoning in Large Output Spaces

Debjyoti Saha Roy

Reasoning-trained language models can pick a document's few labels from thousands of candidates, zero-shot. We ask which components make these choices, and whether a student can be taught where to look as well as what to write.
